# Turkey Trucking Market Research — RoadwiseFleet

**Scope:** Turkish road-freight market and the Turkey–EU corridor · **Data window:** 2023–2025 (some older sources flagged) · **Method:** Turkish- and English-language web research (industry press, UTIKAD/UND reporting, platform FAQs and reports, app-store listings, complaint platforms). Claims without a solid public source are explicitly marked *unverified/qualitative*.

---

## 1. Market facts

**Road freight is the backbone of Turkish exports.** Turkey hit a record in 2024 with **2,095,000 export truck trips** ([UTİKAD](https://www.utikad.org.tr/Detay/Sektor-Haberleri/36850/turkiye-karayolu-tasimaciliginda-yeni-bir-rekora-imza-atti), [Taşıma Dünyası](https://www.tasimadunyasi.com/turkiye-karayolu-tasimaciliginda-yeni-bir-rekora-imza-atti-2024de-2-milyon-95-bin-ihracat-seferi-yapildi), [Karaköse Haber](https://www.karakosehaber.com/turkiye-de-2024-yilinda-2-milyonun-uzerinde-ihracat-seferi-gerceklestirildi/187823/)); 2023 was comparable, with Anadolu Agency reporting **2,095,000 export trips that year too** ([AA](https://www.aa.com.tr/tr/ekonomi/turkiyede-gecen-yil-2-milyon-95-bin-ihracat-seferi-yapildi/3472660)). Roughly **one-third of Turkish export value moves by road** ([UMKE](https://www.umke.org/ihracatimizin-3te-1i-karayolundan)); Turkish hauliers operate into **~70 countries across three continents**, overwhelmingly the EU and Middle East ([UTİKAD](https://www.utikad.org.tr/Detay/Sektor-Haberleri/36672/3-kitada-70-ulkeye-karayolu-tasimaciligi)). The EU is the dominant corridor: Germany, France, Benelux, Italy and the UK are the classic destinations, and EU market softness directly shows up at Turkish borders (see §2).

**Market size and fragmentation.** Tırport's own sector analysis puts daily freight payments in Turkish road haulage at **~$176M/day** ([Tırport](https://tirport.com/turkiyede-karayolu-nakliyesinde-gunde-176-milyon-dolar-navlun-odemesi-yapiliyor/)); the broader Turkish logistics industry is publicly targeting a **$200B market** ([UTİKAD Logistics Summit](https://www.utikad.org.tr/Detay/Sektor-Haberleri/40441/the-logistics-industry-which-aims-a-200-billion-market-size-met-at-turkiye-logistics-summit-2025-)). The registered motor-vehicle fleet exceeds **30 million vehicles** ([Tgrthaber](https://www.tgrthaber.com/ekonomi/trafige-kayitli-toplam-tasit-sayisi-30-milyonu-gecti-vatandas-benzinliden-vazgecmedi-2978772)); within it, the commercial truck (kamyon) segment is roughly **~900k–1M units plus ~4.5M light trucks (kamyonet)** — approximate TÜİK-based breakdown, not a precise count ([RayHaber](https://rayhaber.com/2024/07/30-milyon-tasit-trafikte-haziran-bilgileri-aciklandi/30-milyon-tasit-trafikte-haziran-bilgileri-aciklandi-2-82mgeoi2-jpeg/), [GHG-Türkiye TÜİK data](https://ghg-turkiye.com/ulastirma-platformu?tab=vehicle&chart=vehicle-distribution#vehicle-distribution)). Fleet structure is dominated by **small fleets and owner-operators**: the typical Turkish haulier runs 1–10 trucks, and single-truck "esnaf" (artisan) operators are the sector's cultural core — *qualitative, widely reported in industry press and visible in forum/association rhetoric* (e.g. [Bolge Gazetesi](https://bolgegazetesi.com.tr/sofor-esnafi-yandik-diyor/), Tırport's own fleet-structure data in its [2022 sector report](https://insights.tirport.com/wp-content/uploads/u49w2an/tirport-2022-rapor.pdf)). UND/UTİKAD reporting on the record 2024 volumes frames capacity as mostly SME-driven ([UND](https://www.und.org.tr/medya-detay/undden-haberler/turkiye-karayolu-tasimaciliginda-yeni-bir-rekora-imza-atti)).

**Driver shortage.** The shortage is acute and structural on both sides of the corridor. Turkey itself reports an **"alarm-level" TIR/truck driver deficit** ([Dünya](https://www.dunya.com/ihracat/tir-ve-kamyon-surucu-acigi-alarm-veriyor-haberi-771499)); UTİKAD cites forecasts that **driver need will double by 2028** ([UTİKAD](https://www.utikad.org.tr/Detay/Sektor-Haberleri/35852/kamyon-soforu-ihtiyaci-2028-yilina-kadar-iki-katina-cikacak!)). On the EU side, Germany alone needs ~**120,000 professional drivers** ([UTİKAD](https://www.utikad.org.tr/Detay/Sektor-Haberleri/40485/almanya-120-bin-tir-soforu-ariyor), [Samanyolu Haber](https://www.samanyoluhaber.com/almanya-kara-kara-dusunuyor-120-bin-kamyontir-soforu-acigi-var-haberi/1484185)), and even France struggles to hire truckers at high pay ([Arti33](https://www.arti33.com/fransada-yuksek-maasa-ragmen-tir-soforu-bulunamiyor/)) — context for why Turkish drivers are pulled into EU operations.

**Fuel and toll pressure.** Diesel is the single biggest cost line: industry reporting puts **fuel at ~50% of freight cost** ([Ticaret Gazetesi](https://ticaretgazetesi.com.tr/2024/08/14/navlun-maliyetinin-%50si-mazota-gidiyor/)), and Turkey repeatedly raised fuel taxes (ÖTV) through 2024–2025 ([Dünya](https://www.dunya.com/ekonomi/akaryakita-otv-zammi-benzin-motorin-ve-lpgnin-guncel-fiyatlari-belli-oldu-haberi-758962), [T24](https://t24.com.tr/ekonomi/benzinin-ardindan-motorine-de-buyuk-zam,1228442?_t=1781479087736)); exact per-litre prices are time-sensitive and not stable enough to quote here. Highway/bridge tolls were raised again for 2025 ([NTV](https://www.ntv.com.tr/turkiye/son-dakika-haberi-yeni-yilda-otoyol-ve-kopru-gecis-ucretleri-belli-oldu-2025-yili-otoyol-ve-kopru-gecis-ucretleri,RAZczYiJKE-e1MdKNhy5gg)), and real landed-cost analyses of the Istanbul→EU lane (ferry, tolls, driver costs) show thin margins that any tool must respect ([Flex Logistics](https://flexlogistics.eu/the-istanbul-route-nobody-talks-about-what-it-actually-costs-to-move-goods-from-turkey-int/)).

## 2. Driver pain points (from forums, association statements, press)

**Border waiting is the #1 voiced grievance.** At Kapıkule (Bulgaria border, Turkey's busiest), waits have repeatedly exceeded **90 hours**, with drivers holding press actions ([Başkent Gazete](https://www.baskentgazete.com.tr/kapikulede-bekleme-sureleri-90-saati-asan-tir-soforlerinden-basin-aciklamasi), [Evrensel](https://www.evrensel.net/haber/5978633/kapikule-de-cile-90-saati-asti?utm_source=rss_feed&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=rss_syndication), [Vox Haber](https://voxhaber.net/haber/HZA3AZJR)); after park "sealing" measures, queues reached **7 km** at the same gate ([Patronlar Dünyası](https://www.patronlardunyasi.com/yazdir/parka-muhur-sonrasi-kapikulede-7-kilometrelik-tir-kuyrugu)). Hamzabeyli was explicitly promoted to offload Kapıkule's export traffic ([AA](https://mobil.aa.com.tr/tr/ekonomi/hamzabeyli-tir-gecisinde-kapikulenin-ihracat-yukunu-paylasti/3789959)). When EU demand softens, the symptom is **~8,000 TIRs parked/waiting at borders and parks** ([Patronlar Dünyası](https://www.patronlardunyasi.com/yazdir/ab-ile-ticaret-yavasladi-8-bin-tir-parkta-uyuyor)).

**Visa/Schengen friction is existential for the corridor.** UTİKAD warns visa problems now **threaten Turkey's foreign trade** ([UTİKAD](https://www.utikad.org.tr/Detay/Sektor-Haberleri/36090/vize-sorunu-turkiye-nin-dis-ticaretini-tehdit-ediyor)); Hürriyet reported visa denials **delaying TIR operations** ([Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/ekonomi/vize-sorunu-tirlari-aksatti-42455233)). The workaround is telling: companies hire **green-passport retired civil servants** as "drivers" just to cross borders ([Diken](https://www.diken.com.tr/lojistik-firmasindan-vize-engeline-cozum-yesil-pasaportlu-emekli-memur-calistiriliyor/)).

**TIR parks: scarcity and safety.** In Europe, secure parking remains a known gap ([ZENDEQ](https://www.zendeq.com/safe-and-secure-truck-parking-in-the-eu/), [Rivista Tir on EU Commission findings](https://rivistatir.it/commissione-ue-la-carenza-di-aree-di-sosta-sicure-e-grave-in-tutta-europa/)). Turkish drivers report theft even while asleep at German rest stops ([Arti49](https://www.arti49.com/almanyada-dinlenme-tesisinde-uyuyan-turk-tir-soforunu-ruhu-bile-duymadan-soydular-2392982h.htm)) and cargo theft in Istanbul (Esenyurt) ([Gazete Birlik](https://www.gazetebirlik.com/istanbul-haberleri/istanbulun-esenyurtta-hirsizlik-olayi-soforun-uyumasini-firsat-bilip-tiri-soydular/192238)); some now install **security cameras** against theft and assault ([Yeni Birlik](https://www.gazetebirlik.com/yerel-haberler/tir-soforleri-diken-ustunde-tehlikeye-karsi-guvenlik-kamerasi-kurdular/133507#1)).

**Fuel costs and payments.** Fuel at ~50% of cost ([Ticaret Gazetesi](https://ticaretgazetesi.com.tr/2024/08/14/navlun-maliyetinin-%50si-mazota-gidiyor/)) plus repeated ÖTV hikes ([Dünya](https://www.dunya.com/ekonomi/akaryakita-otv-zammi-benzin-motorin-ve-lpgnin-guncel-fiyatlari-belli-oldu-haberi-758962)) squeeze drivers paid per trip. Payment delays from freight forwarders are a recurring complaint theme in driver/association statements ("ŞOFÖR ESNAFI YANDIK" is a representative headline: [Bolge Gazetesi](https://bolgegazetesi.com.tr/sofor-esnafi-yandik-diyor/); lengthening payment terms across the logistics chain is documented by UTİKAD ([UTİKAD](https://www.utikad.org.tr/Detay/Sektor-Haberleri/36614/lojistik-sektorunde-vadeler-uzadi-mazot-maliyetleri-tavan-yapti)) — *specific driver-level non-payment statistics are unverified*.

**Navigation and workload.** Truck-specific navigation failures injure drivers (a TIR driver "victim of navigation" story is a recurring genre: [Haber7](https://m.haber7.com/guncel/haber/3074124-tir-soforu-navigasyonun-magduru-oldu)). Workload and lifestyle keep the profession short-handed even where pay is high ([Arti33](https://www.arti33.com/fransada-yuksek-maasa-ragmen-tir-soforu-bulunamiyor/)). Equipment grievances (e.g. truck-brand complaints on Şikayetvar, [MAN complaints](https://www.sikayetvar.com/man-kamyon-ve-otobus#1)) show drivers do use Turkish complaint platforms — a channel RoadwiseFleet can monitor.

## 3. Hauler / forwarder pain points

**Empty return legs.** A widely cited Turkish stat: **4 of 10 trucks return empty** ([Ekonomi Gazetesi](https://www.ekonomigazetesi.com/ekonomi/10-kamyondan-4u-bosa-cikti-31900/)); finding return loads from the EU (and matching inbound/outbound) is the classic pain, and algorithm-based empty-return matching is explicitly a national talking point ([Türkiye Gazetesi](https://www.turkiyegazetesi.com.tr/ekonomi/yukunu-algoritmalardan-alacak-nakliye-araclari-bos-donmeyecek-1147440?s=2)).

**Cash flow.** Payment terms across the chain **lengthened while diesel costs peaked** ([UTİKAD](https://www.utikad.org.tr/Detay/Sektor-Haberleri/36614/lojistik-sektorunde-vadeler-uzadi-mazot-maliyetleri-tavan-yapti)) — forwarders pay hauliers late, hauliers pay drivers late. *Broker-commission percentages in Turkey are unverified in public sources*; load boards publish membership fees instead (see §4–5).

**Compliance stack is deep and digitizing fast.** Domestic dispatch is being pushed to **e-İrsaliye (electronic waybill)**, mandatory for expanding segments through 2025 ([Nilvera](https://www.nilvera.com/2025-e-irsaliye-zorunlulugu-kimler-kapsamda-sektorler-ve-sartlar), [Teknoliva](https://www.teknoliva.com/e-irsaliye/), with e-Fatura/e-İrsaliye deadlines like 1 July enforced by tax authorities ([Ekonomist/TÜRMOB](https://www.ekonomist.com.tr/haberler/turmob-dan-e-fatura-uyarisi-son-gun-1-temmuz-75904), [BBDAS July 2025 tax calendar](https://www.bbdas.com.tr/bbdas-01-07-2025-124-temmuz-ayi-vergi-takvimi-g-2747))). International leg: CMR is the standard document; **e-CMR is now legally live in 41 countries** ([TransFollow](https://www.transfollow.com/ecmr-now-live-in-41-countries/)), real e-CMR pilots are running (e.g. [Gruber Logistics test](https://transportjournal.com/2024/10/10/gruber-logistics-successfully-completes-ecmr-test/)), and **Turkey and Azerbaijan agreed to cooperate on e-CMR implementation** ([Ministry of Digital Development, AZ](https://mincom.gov.az/en/media-en/news/azerbaijan-and-turkiye-strengthen-cooperation-on-the-implementation-of-the-e-cmr-system)) — the corridor is moving paperless. Turkish international hauliers also carry the **Uluslararası Karayolu Taşımacılığı (U.K.T.) licence (MIC)** and transit/customs paperwork (TIR carnets, ATA) — *standard regulatory knowledge, cited generally in corridor analyses* ([Flex Logistics](https://flexlogistics.eu/the-istanbul-route-nobody-talks-about-what-it-actually-costs-to-move-goods-from-turkey-int/)); our [gap analysis](gap-analysis.md) flags customs/transit documents as an unserved slice.

**Border unpredictability.** Border waits (90h+ at Kapıkule, §2) directly destroy forwarder service levels — real-time gate/queue information is an operational need, not a luxury.

## 4. Digitalization status

**Local load platforms are real but partial.** The Turkish digital-freight-matching market is recognized as its own market with local champions ([Research and Markets](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/6105208/digital-freight-matching-market-in-turkey#1)). **Tırport** is the flagship: a load board + SaaS suite ("virtual logistics manager"), reporting ~$2M funding in 2024 ([Webrazzi](https://webrazzi.com/2024/03/28/dijital-lojistik-platformu-tirport-finberg-liderliginde-yaklasik-2-milyon-dolar-yatirim-aldi/)), 10x+ pandemic-era volume growth ([RayHaber](https://rayhaber.com/2021/08/tirport-pandemide-is-hacmini-10-kattan-fazla-artirdi/)), a 2022 sector report ([Tırport 2022](https://insights.tirport.com/wp-content/uploads/u49w2an/tirport-2022-rapor.pdf)), and a **driver-side app, yükCEPte** ([Tırport](https://tirport.com/en/load-finder-app/), [Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.abplus.tirport&hl=tr)). It monetizes via membership plans (details in its [FAQ](https://tirport.com/sik-sorulan-sorular/)). **Navlungo** is more a **digital forwarder for cross-border/e-commerce parcels** than a truck load board ([WORLDEF](https://worldef.net/dosyalar/uluslararasi-lojistigi-dijitallestiren-navlungo/), [n24 bilgi](https://n24.com.tr/bilgi/navlungo/?amp=1)) — it became eBay's official logistics partner ([bthaber](https://bthaber.com/haberler/navlungo-ebayin-resmi-lojistik-partneri-oldu)) and keeps expanding branch coverage ([Lojiport](https://www.lojiport.com/navlungo-simdi-denizlide-115667h.htm)) — but attracts billing complaints on Şikayetvar ([Şikayetvar Navlungo](https://www.sikayetvar.com/navlungo/navlungoda-haksiz-52000-tllik-fatura-ve-tekrarlayan-ucret-talebi#1)). **Yolda** supplies transport technology; e.g. Murat Lojistik digitized its operations with Yolda ([Taşıma Dünyası](https://www.tasimadunyasi.com/murat-lojistik-yolda-teknolojisi-ile-dijitallesiyor), [Taşıyanlar](https://tasiyanlar.com/en-yeniler/murat-lojistik-dijitallesiyor.html)). **KargomKolay** is again **cross-border parcel shipping** (a Shopify app), not trucking ([Shopify](https://apps.shopify.com/kargomkolay?locale=tr#1)). Classifieds like nakliyeilani.com/NakliyeNet operate as simple load boards ([nakliyeilani.com](https://nakliyeilani.com/dis-ticaret-firmalar/5866A5C8-F275-41D4-B05F-58FAF01176C3/)).

**WhatsApp and Excel remain the real "TMS".** Trucker WhatsApp groups are a known informal channel for loads and gossip — *documented only anecdotally (e.g. [Yandex Q&A](https://yandex.com.tr/yacevap/c/otomobil/q/kamyoncular-whatsapp-grubu-var-mi-2615326159)); treat as qualitative but strongly supported by market commentary*. The 40%-empty-return stat ([Ekonomi Gazetesi](https://www.ekonomigazetesi.com/ekonomi/10-kamyondan-4u-bosa-cikti-31900/)) itself implies most matching still happens manually/offline.

**Driver-app landscape is thin.** The EU incumbents exist in Turkish app stores — **Trucker Path Europe** ([Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sixdays.truckerpath.eu&hl=tr)), **Truckfly** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ie/app/truckfly-truck-gps/id1050082939), [reviews](https://chrome-stats.com/d/com.truckfly.truckfly/reviews)) — plus **Trans.eu's driver apps** ([Play Store TR](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=eu.trans.loads2do&hl=tr)); locally, a crop of new Turkish apps (Lojistikcim, Yük Merkezi, YukleGel — all appearing in app stores in late 2025 ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lojistikcim/id6755790900), [App Store TR](https://apps.apple.com/tr/app/y%C3%BCk-merkezi-nakliye-y%C3%BCk-bul/id6756026367), [App Store TW](https://apps.apple.com/tw/app/yuklegel/id6758655200))) signals demand but no dominant driver-operational winner.

**Adoption barriers.** Complaints about platform billing ([Şikayetvar Navlungo](https://www.sikayetvar.com/navlungo/navlungoda-haksiz-52000-tllik-fatura-ve-tekrarlayan-ucret-talebi#1)), low satisfaction with EU load boards (TIMOCOM scores ~2.8/5 on Trustpilot: [ES](https://es.trustpilot.com/review/www.timocom.com), [JP](https://jp.trustpilot.com/review/www.timocom.com#1)), membership-fee models that punish casual users, language gaps, and fragmented point solutions (loads here, navigation there, e-irsaliye elsewhere) are the barriers to broad digitization.

**Pattern:** load exchanges and forwarders sell *loads*; telematics sell *tracking*; nobody serves the **Turkish SME haulier's day-to-day operation** — e-İrsaliye/e-CMR creation, border/parking intelligence, payment status, tacho, truck-safe navigation — in one Turkish-first driver app with a cheap dispatcher dashboard. This mirrors the gap identified in our [EU research](europe-market-research.md) and [gap analysis](gap-analysis.md).

## 6. Opportunities for RoadwiseFleet in Turkey and on TR–EU corridors

1. **Forced digitization trigger.** e-İrsaliye mandates through 2025 ([Nilvera](https://www.nilvera.com/2025-e-irsaliye-zorunlulugu-kimler-kapsamda-sektorler-ve-sartlar)) + e-CMR momentum on the corridor ([mincom](https://mincom.gov.az/en/media-en/news/azerbaijan-and-turkiye-strengthen-cooperation-on-the-implementation-of-the-e-cmr-system), [TransFollow](https://www.transfollow.com/ecmr-now-live-in-41-countries/)) create a *must-buy* compliance moment for the same SMEs who currently run WhatsApp/Excel. A driver-side e-İrsaliye/e-CMR capture that flows to the dispatcher is the wedge feature no competitor bundles.
2. **Driver-first Android gap.** Turkish drivers live in Android; incumbents (Tırport yükCEPte, Trucker Path, Truckfly) are load/nav-only or membership-gated. A free, Turkish-first driver app — truck-safe navigation, border queue status (Kapıkule/Hamzabeyli 90h+ waits: [Evrensel](https://www.evrensel.net/haber/5978633/kapikule-de-cile-90-saati-asti)), secure-parking alerts, rest-time/tacho assistant, dispatcher chat, payment tracking — attacks the highest-frequency pain with the lowest adoption barrier.
3. **Return-load matching for the corridor.** 40% empty returns ([Ekonomi Gazetesi](https://www.ekonomigazetesi.com/ekonomi/10-kamyondan-4u-bosa-cikti-31900/)) is the monetizable load-side hook; pair it with border-side intelligence (wait times, park/seal situations) that forwarders also pay for.
4. **Cash-flow visibility.** Lengthened payment terms ([UTİKAD](https://www.utikad.org.tr/Detay/Sektor-Haberleri/36614/lojistik-sektorunde-vadeler-uzadi-mazot-maliyetleri-tavan-yapti)) and driver payment-delay complaints (§2) → invoice/freight-payment status for drivers + factoring-lite later.
5. **Safety layer.** Camera-use and theft reports (§2) → parking-safety ratings and alerts as a trust differentiator in a market where drivers already fear rest stops ([Arti49](https://www.arti49.com/almanyada-dinlenme-tesisinde-uyuyan-turk-tir-soforunu-ruhu-bile-duymadan-soydular-2392982h.htm)).
6. **Language/UX fit.** Turkish-first UI (TR/EN/DE/RU for the corridor), low data usage, offline-tolerant design, and per-truck pricing that matches a 1–10-truck esnaf fleet's budget (EU TMS benchmarks ~$19–469/mo, typical ~$127/mo ([CostBench](https://costbench.com/software/trucking-tms/)) — Turkey must price far below that).

## Summary — the best entry wedge

The single best entry wedge in Turkey is **a free, driver-first Turkish Android app that fixes the daily operating life of the Turkish TIR driver on the TR–EU corridor — border/park intelligence (Kapıkule/Hamzabeyli wait times, secure-parking safety), truck-safe navigation, e-İrsaliye/e-CMR document capture, rest-time and payment tracking, and direct dispatcher chat — monetized through a cheap dispatcher SaaS for the 1–20-truck hauler that the app feeds (loads, documents, tracking, analytics)**. This exploits the exact gap the local load boards (Tırport) and EU exchanges (Trans.eu, TIMOCOM) leave open: they sell loads or tracking, not the driver's operational cockpit, while e-İrsaliye (2025) and e-CMR (41 countries and counting) force the same SMEs off WhatsApp/Excel into compliant digital workflows — a timing window that makes a driver-first wedge both cheap to acquire and sticky to retain.
