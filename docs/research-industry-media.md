# European Road Freight 2024–2025: Industry State, Problems, and the Small-Carrier Reality

*Market research compiled for RoadwiseFleet. Every claim carries an inline source link. All figures are as reported by industry media, associations, and analysts in 2024–2025 unless dated otherwise.*

---

## 1. Hard numbers

### Market size and structure
- The Europe road freight transport market was estimated at **~US$497 billion in 2024**, heading to ~US$602 billion by 2030 ([Mordor Intelligence via GII Research](https://www.giiresearch.com/report/moi1692139-europe-road-freight-transport-market-share.html)).
- The EU sector counts **>1 million operators** running **>7 million heavy trucks/buses/coaches** and ~35 million vans ([Motor a Diesel](https://motoradiesel.com/2025/02/el-transporte-en-la-union-europea-cuenta-con-mas-de-1-millon-de-operadores/)).
- **89% of EU truck operators are SMEs** ([IRU/Upply/Ti, Q4 2024](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/european-road-freight-rates-q4-2024-contract-and-spot-rates-edge)); **~98% of EU road-freight enterprises employ fewer than 50 people** and account for ~79% of the workforce ([IRU Global Driver Shortage Report 2025](https://www.iru.org/sites/default/files/2026-06/IRU%20Intelligence%20Briefing%20preview%20-%20Global%20Driver%20Shortage%20Report%202025.pdf)).

### Bankruptcies / insolvency wave
- **Germany: 23,900 business insolvencies in 2025 (+8.3% y-o-y), a 10-year peak**, with transport & logistics among the hardest hit: 11.3 insolvencies per 10,000 firms and 392 firms at extreme risk; forecast ~24,450 cases in 2026. In H1 2024 transport already had the **highest insolvency rate of any sector (60.9 per 10,000 firms)**, above construction ([Uomini e Trasporti](https://www.uominietrasporti.it/professione/finanza-e-mercato/germania-la-locomotiva-sbuffa-nel-2025-record-di-fallimenti-nei-trasporti-e-nella-logistica/); [IfM Bonn](https://www.ifm-bonn.org/en/press/press-releases/meldung/the-risk-of-insolvency-was-the-highest-in-the-transport-and-logistics-sector-in-2024)).
- **France: 809 transport-sector failures in Q2 2025 (+13%), the highest since 2020** — "every working day, ten road freight companies disappear in France" (Union TLF, citing Altares). Average sector margin is only **2–3%, "by far the lowest in Europe"** ([Le Journal du Poids Lourd](https://journaldupoidslourd.com/transport/union-tlf-chaque-jour-dix-entreprises-de-transport-de-marchandises-disparaissent-en-france/)).
- **Poland: the ZMPD association called 2024 "the blackest year in the history of the industry"** ([ZMPD](https://zmpd.pl/aktualnosc.php?akt_id=2725)); third-country drivers in Poland fell by ~12,000 in 2024 ([Log24](https://log24.pl/news/7-na-10-przewoznikow-w-europie-ma-trudnosci-ze-znalezieniem-kierowcow/)).
- **UK (context): haulage insolvencies peaked in 2023 and have since fallen**, per official figures to Parliament ([Motor Transport](https://motortransport.co.uk/figures-suggest-haulage-insolvencies-have-peaked/89235.article)).

### Driver shortage (per market)
- **Europe: 502,000 unfilled truck-driver positions in 2025, a 13% vacancy rate** — up from 426,000 in the 2024 survey ([IRU Global Driver Shortage Report 2025](https://www.iru.org/sites/default/files/2026-06/IRU%20Intelligence%20Briefing%20preview%20-%20Global%20Driver%20Shortage%20Report%202025.pdf); [IRU rates briefing Q2 2025](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/european-road-freight-rates-converge)).
- The shortage is **skewed to small firms: 15% vacancy among <50-employee operators vs 9% for 50+**, and small operators have an older workforce (45% of drivers aged 55+, vs 37% at large firms); **660,500 European driver retirements are projected over the next five years** ([IRU Global Driver Shortage Report 2025](https://www.iru.org/sites/default/files/2026-06/IRU%20Intelligence%20Briefing%20preview%20-%20Global%20Driver%20Shortage%20Report%202025.pdf)).
- **Germany: 100,000+ missing drivers in 2025, rising to 120,000+ in 2026 (BGL)**; a structural deficit of ~15,000 drivers/year (30–35k retire, 15–20k enter) ([Eurotransport](https://www.eurotransport.de/logistik/spedition-und-logistik/fachkraeftemangel-haelt-an-bald-fehlen-120-000-berufskraftfahrer/)). **Spain: ~30,000 missing** ([Antena 3](https://www.antena3.com/noticias/economia/crisis-camioneros-espana-agrava-faltarian-alrededor-30000_2026040469d16a0e83aca52e0e430abe.html)). **70% of European carriers reported difficulty filling driver posts** in 2024 ([Log24/IRU](https://log24.pl/news/7-na-10-przewoznikow-w-europie-ma-trudnosci-ze-znalezieniem-kierowcow/)).
- Demographics: only **3.2% of drivers are women and 4% are under 25**; ~550,000 drivers expected to retire by 2029 ([IRU, Transport Logistic Munich](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/insights-ground-ai-and-leading-trends-trucking)).

### Fuel, tolls, and cost indices
- **Diesel**: EU average fell to €1.50/L in Sept 2024, rebounded to €1.57/L by 31 Dec 2024 (+4.6%); Q2 2025 prices were down 6.4% q-o-q ([IRU Q4 2024](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/european-road-freight-rates-q4-2024-contract-and-spot-rates-edge); [IRU Q2 2025](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/european-road-freight-rates-converge)).
- **Germany toll**: a CO₂ component was added 1 Dec 2023 (pricing EU CO₂ at €200/t), pushing **operator costs up by up to 83%** ([Ti](https://ti-insight.com/briefs/co2-toll-in-germany-to-increase-operator-costs-by-up-to-83-from-december-2023/)). From 1 July 2024 the toll was **extended to all commercial vehicles >3.5 t**; a typical Euro 6 articulated truck in CO₂ class 1 pays **34.8 cents/km** (CO₂ component alone ≈15.8 cents/km), i.e. ~€34,800/yr at 100,000 km ([Impargo](https://impargo.de/en/blog/toll-collect-toll-rate-with-co2-emission-tax-2024)).
- **Total cost of ownership in Germany rose 24% between 2021 and 2024** (tolls +83% in 2023, fuel +19%, wages up, truck purchase prices nearly doubled over a decade) ([IRU Munich](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/insights-ground-ai-and-leading-trends-trucking)).
- **Other toll hikes Q3 2025**: Romania +17.8%, Bulgaria +7.7%, Slovakia +40.9% (plus VAT) ([IRU Q3 2025](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/european-road-freight-contract-and-spot-rates-rise-tandem)).

### Rates trends
- Upply–Ti–IRU benchmark: **Q4 2024 contract index 128.9 (+2.8 q-o-q, −1.4 y-o-y); spot 123.9 (+0.5, −1.0)** ([IRU Q4 2024](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/european-road-freight-rates-q4-2024-contract-and-spot-rates-edge)). **Q2 2025: both indices converged at 132.2**, spot at its lowest since Q4 2023 ([IRU Q2 2025](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/european-road-freight-rates-converge)). **Q3 2025: spot 134.3 (+1.9 y-o-y), contract 134.0 (+3.1 y-o-y)** ([IRU Q3 2025](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/european-road-freight-contract-and-spot-rates-rise-tandem)).
- The story behind the indices: **rates rose or held while costs rose faster — margins shrank**; carriers were "busy enough to keep trucks on the road, but not profitable enough to build reserves" ([SCS Bureau](https://scsbureau.com/2025/10/07/europes-road-freight-providers-can-finally-exhale-but-are-the-hard-times-really-over/)).

### Payment terms
- **Spain: average payment terms hit a record 62 days in early 2025** (the legal cap is 60), and ~60% of companies still breach the late-payment law ([Fenadismer](https://www.fenadismerencarretera.com/los-plazos-de-pago-baten-un-nuevo-record-y-se-situan-en-62-dias-de-media/); [Nexotrans](https://www.nexotrans.com/noticia/115764/nexotrans/el-60-de-empresas-incumple-la-ley-de-morosidad.html)).
- **Germany: unpaid receivables in the sector reached €32.4 billion in H1 2024**, a liquidity squeeze along the whole chain ([Uomini e Trasporti](https://www.uominietrasporti.it/professione/finanza-e-mercato/germania-la-locomotiva-sbuffa-nel-2025-record-di-fallimenti-nei-trasporti-e-nella-logistica/)).

### Empty kilometres
- **Over 20% of all truck-kilometres driven in Europe are empty runs** (Eurostat data) ([TruckFocus](https://truckfocus.pl/nowosci/77048/taki-jest-odsetek-pustych-przejazdow-w-transporcie-jak-mozna-temu-zaradzic); [ManagerPlus](https://managerplus.pl/ponad-20-kilometrow-w-europejskim-transporcie-drogowym-to-puste-przebiegi-21041)).

## 2. What industry bodies say the top 5 problems are

Synthesising IRU, UETR, BGL, Union TLF, and national associations (2024–25), the recurring top problems are:

1. **Driver shortage (structural, demographic).** IRU calls it "a major challenge... putting significant pressure on road transport capacity", with 426–502k unfilled positions and ~550k retirements by 2029 ([IRU Munich](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/insights-ground-ai-and-leading-trends-trucking)); BGL warns the gap is 100k→120k+ in Germany ([Eurotransport](https://www.eurotransport.de/logistik/spedition-und-logistik/fachkraeftemangel-haelt-an-bald-fehlen-120-000-berufskraftfahrer/)); UETR lists "mano de obra" (labour) among its four challenges ([Ruta del Transporte/UETR manifesto](https://www.rutadeltransporte.com/noticias-transporte/transporte-carretera-europa-manifiesto-retos_0_1890410952.html)).
2. **Cost inflation and margin compression.** IRU's Vincent Erard: "Truck operators, 89% of which are SMEs in the EU, continue to face soaring costs, further squeezing their thin margins. Rising toll charges, driver shortages and new EU regulations... are pushing both operating costs and capital investments ever higher" ([IRU Q4 2024](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/european-road-freight-rates-q4-2024-contract-and-spot-rates-edge)). Union TLF: margins of 2–3%, "by far the lowest in Europe" ([JPL](https://journaldupoidslourd.com/transport/union-tlf-chaque-jour-dix-entreprises-de-transport-de-marchandises-disparaissent-en-france/)).
3. **Unfair competition and uneven enforcement of EU social rules.** UETR's manifesto calls for "un mercado europeo con menos competencia desleal" (less unfair competition) and better compliance with EU social legislation ([Cadena de Suministro](https://www.cadenadesuministro.es/transporte-carretera/pequenos-transportistas-europeos-reclaman-mercado-europeo-con-menos-competencia-desleal-homogeneo_1501922_102.html); [Ruta del Transporte](https://www.rutadeltransporte.com/noticias-transporte/transporte-carretera-europa-manifiesto-retos_0_1890410952.html)).
4. **The green transition landing on SMEs with no support.** IRU's EU manifesto priority #1 is supporting the transition to carbon neutrality "while maintaining operational efficiency, resilience and competitiveness"; IRU says "operators are not receiving the decarbonisation support they need" ([IRU Manifesto 2024–29](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/iru-frames-key-priorities-new-european-parliament-drive-eu-forward); [IRU Munich](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/insights-ground-ai-and-leading-trends-trucking)). UETR demands "apoyo a la transición ecológica de las pymes" ([Ruta del Transporte](https://www.rutadeltransporte.com/noticias-transporte/transporte-carretera-europa-manifiesto-retos_0_1890410952.html)).
5. **Compliance burden and fiscal/regulatory environment.** Union TLF: the situation "commands awareness of the fiscal and regulatory environment that harms the competitiveness of the French flag" ([JPL](https://journaldupoidslourd.com/transport/union-tlf-chaque-jour-dix-entreprises-de-transport-de-marchandises-disparaissent-en-france/)); IRU's third manifesto priority is to "digitalise the sector in a smart way" while the driver shortage "is already impacting the European economy" ([IRU](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/iru-frames-key-priorities-new-european-parliament-drive-eu-forward)).

## 3. Regulatory timeline 2025–2027 — and what each forces a small carrier to do

| When | Rule | What it forces small carriers to buy/do |
|---|---|---|
| **19 Aug 2025 (done)** | **Smart Tachograph 2 (G2V2) retrofit** mandatory for all HDVs in international traffic still on first-gen smart tachos (after grace periods for earlier milestones) ([IRU](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/eu-road-transport-ready-tomorrows-smart-tacho-deadline)). | ~€500–1,500 per vehicle for device + workshop fit; booking workshops, route planning around downtime ([Stoneridge](https://stoneridge-tachographs.com/en/news-events/avoid-fines-and-risks-retrofit-your-tachograph-today)). |
| **1 July 2026** | Smart Tacho 2 plus **driving/rest-time and driver-posting rules extended to 2.5–3.5 t vans in international services** ([IRU smart tacho article](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/eu-road-transport-ready-tomorrows-smart-tacho-deadline)). | Van fleets need tacho installations, compliance systems, driver-card management — a whole new segment of buyers for compliance tech. |
| **1 July 2024 → ongoing** | **CO₂-based tolls** (Germany Dec 2023 + July 2024 extension to >3.5 t; many other states raising tolls) ([Impargo](https://impargo.de/en/blog/toll-collect-toll-rate-with-co2-emission-tax-2024); [Ti](https://ti-insight.com/briefs/co2-toll-in-germany-to-increase-operator-costs-by-up-to-83-from-december-2023/); [IRU Q3 2025](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/european-road-freight-contract-and-spot-rates-rise-tandem)). | Toll-box/EETS devices, per-route toll calculation, cost pass-through clauses; older (CO₂-class 1) trucks pay more — pressure to renew fleets. |
| **9 Jan 2025 / Sept 2025 / Jan 2026 / 9 July 2027** | **eFTI (Regulation (EU) 2020/1056)**: implementing acts in force Jan 2025; specs Sept 2025; platforms can prepare from Jan 2026; **from 9 July 2027 all Member State authorities must accept digital freight information** via certified platforms — paper can no longer be demanded when an eFTI record exists ([eFTI4ALL](https://efti4all.eu/eu-acts-enforced-efti-progresses/); [Planlogi](https://planlogi.com/eng/ecmr-efti-2027-guide)). | Carriers must adopt **certified eFTI platforms/eCMR** (subscription + data integration); "carriers who wait will be told by their customers which platform to use" ([Planlogi](https://planlogi.com/eng/ecmr-efti-2027-guide)). eCMR today is used in **<1% of ~280M annual international CMR operations**, and interoperability is unsolved ([IRU](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/ecmr-road-wider-adoption)). |
| **29 Nov 2026 (cars/vans) / 2028 (trucks)** | **Euro 7** (Regulation (EU) 2024/1257): stricter limits, durability and real-world monitoring; for heavy vehicles from 2028 ([EFE](https://efe.com/economia/2023-12-18/euro-7-ue-emisiones-coches/); [Regulation text](https://admin.lawthek.eu/detail/b8432a14-d742-4e40-99e2-8219d82f215f/en/)). | New-vehicle purchases become more expensive (Euro 7 + GSR2 safety mandates add thousands per unit); small carriers defer fleet renewal, keeping older, toll-penalised trucks. |
| **2022 → enforcing 2025–26** | **Mobility Package** (posting of drivers, cabotage rules, return-of-vehicle) — enforcement intensifying, now backed by G2V2 remote data ([IRU](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/eu-road-transport-ready-tomorrows-smart-tacho-deadline)). | Administrative overhead per cross-border trip; dispatch software that tracks posting/cabotage limits becomes effectively mandatory for international SMEs. |
| **2030 (target)** | European Commission's "paperless cabin" target — IRU: "we are still far from" it ([IRU](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/eu-road-transport-ready-tomorrows-smart-tacho-deadline)). | Everything above, accelerated. |

## 4. Digitalization reality: adoption among small fleets

- **eCMR is used in under 1% of international road-freight operations** despite 36 countries ratifying the protocol; blockers are interoperability, enforcement recognition, unclear signature rules, and "limited availability of reliable eCMR platforms" ([IRU](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/ecmr-road-wider-adoption)).
- **AI: only ~2% of European road-transport companies have implemented AI**, and ~90% of small companies (<49 employees — i.e. ~98% of operators) are not using it at all (IRU survey of 500+ operators in 19 countries, per ANETRA) ([ANETRA](https://anetra-informa.es/noticias/actualidad-del-sector/anetra-constanta-que-solo-un-2-de-las-empresas-europeas-del-transporte-por-carretera-han-implantado-tecnologias-de-ia/)).
- **Core workflows are still manual**: only 29% of logistics companies have digital tools across core operational workflows; 61% still run on email/spreadsheets; 57% have suffered shipment delays from document errors; 47% cite legacy-system integration as the top barrier — "digital maturity... depends less on owning technology and more on connecting it" (Deep Current survey) ([The Loadstar](https://theloadstar.com/ai-is-needed-in-logistics-but-it-can-be-a-multiplier-or-a-liability/)).
- **Investment intent is there but concentrated upstream**: 21.5% of road-freight firms say digitalisation is their top 12-month investment; 18.4% plan data analytics/real-time visibility; 12.8% plan digital freight platforms — and most platform development is happening at the large forwarder end (Ti State of Logistics Survey 2024) ([Ti SOLS 2024](https://www.ti-insight.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Road-Transport-SOLS-2024-WP-3.pdf)).
- **Why SMEs lag**: cost and time (two-person fleets can't staff a TMS migration), legacy/paper workflow inertia, fear of enforcement-uncertain tech (eCMR), and the more urgent driver problem consuming all attention; only ~10% of small businesses even use their vehicle data (Ford Pro study) ([vision-mobility](https://vision-mobility.de/cn/news/futeproyanjiu-jinyou10-dexiaoxingqiyeliyongcheliangshuju-387036.html); [IRU eCMR](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/ecmr-road-wider-adoption)).

## 5. Consolidation and market-structure change

- **Platforms buying carriers' books**: digital forwarder **sennder took over Uber Freight's European business, then closed the acquisition of C.H. Robinson's European Surface Transportation operations (Feb 2025)**, becoming one of the top-5 European full-truckload players across 20+ markets ([Transport Info](https://www.transportinfo.fr/plateforme-digitale-sennder-acquiert-la-branche-transport-europeenne-de-c-h-robinson/); [sennder](https://www.sennder.com/en/press/sennder-announces-successful-closing-of-the-acquisition-of-the-european-surface-transportation-operations-of-c-h-robinson)).
- **Freight-tech consolidation**: DAT agreed to acquire the **Convoy platform from Flexport** — "freight tech's biggest shakeup of 2025" ([Ti](https://ti-insight.com/briefs/dat-acquires-convoy-platform-in-freight-techs-biggest-shakeup-of-2025/)).
- **Forwarder mega-merger**: DSV completed its ~€14B acquisition of **DB Schenker**, creating the world's largest freight forwarder, with knock-on rationalisation of European road assets ([TT](https://www.ttnews.com/articles/dsv-seeks-usa-truck-buyer); [DSV](https://dsv.gcs-web.com/static-files/452d9cee-09be-415c-ab4c-dd0341fbd4a7)).
- **Market-structure squeeze on owner-operators**: German insolvencies are driving "market consolidation, with the exit of the weakest firms and strengthening of structured operators" ([Uomini e Trasporti](https://www.uominietrasporti.it/professione/finanza-e-mercato/germania-la-locomotiva-sbuffa-nel-2025-record-di-fallimenti-nei-trasporti-e-nella-logistica/)); in Italy ~1% of companies already control a third of demand ([Uomini e Trasporti](https://www.uominietrasporti.it/professione/finanza-e-mercato/germania-la-locomotiva-sbuffa-nel-2025-record-di-fallimenti-nei-trasporti-e-nella-logistica/)). Broker/platform intermediation adds margin layers on top of 2–3% carrier margins (Union TLF's France figure) ([JPL](https://journaldupoidslourd.com/transport/union-tlf-chaque-jour-dix-entreprises-de-transport-de-marchandises-disparaissent-en-france/)).

## 6. Contrarian findings — what contradicts the startup narrative

1. **Digital freight "first wave" largely failed.** The Loadstar: "The first wave of digital logistics struggles was characterised by distress, selling off, and bankruptcies, like the insolvency of Instafreight, and the collapse of Convoy, a company that had raised $1bn and was valued at $3.8bn"; the second wave is about "scale requir[ing] resources beyond the capability of these companies, and... patience, which most shareholders of VC-backed firms lack" ([The Loadstar](https://theloadstar.com/its-a-struggle-for-digital-forwarders-with-poster-boy-flexport-losing-value/)). Flexport itself missed its 2024 profitability target ([The Loadstar](https://theloadstar.com/its-a-struggle-for-digital-forwarders-with-poster-boy-flexport-losing-value/)).
2. **The market doesn't behave like an "app problem".** Deep Current: after a decade of investment, freight still runs on a "surprisingly manual operating model"; employees are the integration layer between fragmented systems ([The Loadstar](https://theloadstar.com/ai-is-needed-in-logistics-but-it-can-be-a-multiplier-or-a-liability/)). eCMR adoption under 1% after 8+ years of legal availability ([IRU](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/ecmr-road-wider-adoption)).
3. **Rates rising ≠ industry healthy.** Ti/Upply/IRU: even as indices rose, carriers' cost base rose faster; margin compression and insolvencies continued — "carriers weren't celebrating" ([SCS Bureau](https://scsbureau.com/2025/10/07/europes-road-freight-providers-can-finally-exhale-but-are-the-hard-times-really-over/); [Upply](https://market-insights.upply.com/en/european-road-freight-modest-price-increases-in-q4-2024)).
4. **The crisis is not uniform.** UK haulage insolvencies peaked in 2023 and are falling ([Motor Transport](https://motortransport.co.uk/figures-suggest-haulage-insolvencies-have-peaked/89235.article)); some segments (e.g. big forwarders) are consolidating profitably — Motor Transport's Top 100 2025 headline is "Consolidation boosts profit".
5. **Associations say the biggest lever isn't tech — it's policy.** IRU's EU priorities are decarbonisation support, smart digitalisation, and the driver shortage ([IRU](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/iru-frames-key-priorities-new-european-parliament-drive-eu-forward)); IRU also criticises the Commission for **excluding transport operators from the Strategic Dialogue on the car industry** — the operators who actually buy the trucks ([Motor a Diesel](https://motoradiesel.com/2025/02/el-transporte-en-la-union-europea-cuenta-con-mas-de-1-millon-de-operadores/)). UETR's manifesto leads with fair competition and SME support, not software ([Ruta del Transporte](https://www.rutadeltransporte.com/noticias-transporte/transporte-carretera-europa-manifiesto-retos_0_1890410952.html)).

## What the data says is the single most urgent problem to solve in 2025–2026

The numbers converge on one answer: **the driver shortage — specifically, its crushing asymmetry on small fleets.** Europe sits at 502,000 unfilled driver positions (13%), but small operators (<50 employees — ~98% of the industry) face 15% vacancy against 9% for large firms, with 45% of their drivers aged 55+, 660,500 retirements due within five years, and essentially no young or female inflow (4% under 25; 3.2% women) ([IRU Global Driver Shortage Report 2025](https://www.iru.org/sites/default/files/2026-06/IRU%20Intelligence%20Briefing%20preview%20-%20Global%20Driver%20Shortage%20Report%202025.pdf); [IRU Munich](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/insights-ground-ai-and-leading-trends-trucking)). Every other headline problem — margin compression (2–3%), the insolvency wave (Germany +8.3%, France +13%), 20% empty kilometres, 62-day payment terms — is either caused or amplified by the fact that the smallest carriers cannot staff, retain, or replace the drivers who generate their only revenue. Freight volumes are projected to grow 9% by 2030 while the driver pool shrinks ([IRU Munich](https://www.iru.org/news-resources/newsroom/insights-ground-ai-and-leading-trends-trucking)), so for 2025–2026, the most urgent problem to solve is the small-fleet driver crisis: retention, recruitment, and making one-truck/one-driver economics survivable — because a carrier that cannot crew its trucks cannot buy your software either.
