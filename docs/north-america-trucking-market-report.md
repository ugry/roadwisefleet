# North America Road Freight Market Research — RoadwiseFleet Opportunity Report

*Prepared for RoadwiseFleet (roadwisefleet.com). Data sources: 2023–2025 industry reports, government statistics (FMCSA, BTS, DOT), trade press, and competitor filings. All statistics cited inline; where a figure could not be verified, the closest reliable source figure is given and flagged.*

---

## 1. Market Overview

**The US trucking market is roughly a $1 trillion-dollar industry.** ATA's *American Trucking Trends* reports trucking generates about **$940 billion in annual gross freight revenue** and moves **~72.6% of all freight tonnage** (~11 billion tons) in the US — for-hire and private fleets combined — and trucking employs roughly **3.5 million professional drivers** plus ~8 million people industry-wide ([trucking.org](https://www.trucking.org/news-insights/ata-releases-latest-edition-american-trucking-trends-0), [motor.com](https://www.motor.com/2025/09/ata-releases-latest-edition-of-american-trucking-trends-2/)). ATA projects freight volumes rebound in 2025 after a 2024 contraction ([trucking.org](https://www.trucking.org/news-insights/truck-freight-bounce-back-2025-ata-projects)).

**Fleet structure is hyper-fragmented.** FMCSA data show **95.5% of US carriers operate 10 or fewer trucks** and 99.6% operate fewer than 100 ([motor.com](https://www.motor.com/category/fleets/page/10/)); ~600,000+ active carriers are registered ([yourerielawyers.com](https://www.yourerielawyers.com/faqs/how-many-trucking-companies-are-there-in-the-u-s/)). Owner-operators and small fleets "remain the lifeblood" of the market through downturns ([freightwaves.com](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/small-carriers-owner-operators-remain-lifeblood-of-transportation-during-market-downturn)).

**Driver shortage: the debate is "quality vs. quantity."** ATA's chief economist pegged the driver gap at a historic high near **80,000 in 2021**, easing to ~**60,000 in 2023–24** ([trucking.org](https://www.trucking.org/news-insights/ata-chief-economist-pegs-driver-shortage-historic-high), [truckingdive.com](https://www.truckingdive.com/news/truck-driver-shortage-2023-ata-conference-costello/696710/)). Critics note the shortage is really about **quality and retention**, not raw numbers ([ccjdigital.com](https://www.ccjdigital.com/regulations/safety-compliance/article/15770320/trucking-has-a-quality-driver-shortage-not-quantity), [landline.media](https://landline.media/magazine/will-ata-give-up-on-driver-shortage-myth/)). Labor and workforce evolution is trucking's defining challenge ([trucordia.com](https://www.trucordia.com/blog/labor-and-workforce-evolution-truckings-defining-challenge)).

**Canada and Mexico.** Canada's long-distance trucking sector is a ~CAD 20B+ industry employing ~300,000 drivers, moving the majority of Canada–US surface trade by value ([ibisworld.org](https://www.ibisworld.org/canada/industry/long-distance-freight-trucking/1150/), [dolphindelivery.ca](https://dolphindelivery.ca/things-to-know-about-canadian-trucking-industry/), [fleetnerd.io](https://fleetnerd.io/canada-trucking-statistics/)). Mexico's CANACAR reports a **90,000-operator deficit** ([imagendeveracruz.mx](https://imagendeveracruz.mx/veracruz/autotransporte-de-carga-en-mexico-enfrenta-deficit-de-90-mil-operadores-jovenes-no-quieren-ser-traileros-canacar-20260728-0038.html), [cronista.com](https://www.cronista.com/mexico/finanzas-economia/el-costo-de-no-tener-choferes-90000-camiones-parados-en-mexico-obligan-a-qualitas-y-canacar-a-un-rescate-urgente/)), driven by insecurity and nearshoring demand ([razon.com.mx](https://www.razon.com.mx/negocios/2024/10/22/inseguridad-carretera-deja-deficit-de-56-mil-operadores-repuntan-costos-de-seguros/)).

**Cross-border USMCA volume is the region's growth engine.** Trucks hauled the **majority of $1.57 trillion in land-border trade in 2023** ([thetrucker.com](https://www.thetrucker.com/trucking-news/business/trucks-hauled-majority-of-1-trillion-in-trade-across-land-borders)); US–Mexico truck freight set record values — monthly cross-border autotransporte value repeatedly topping **$50 billion** in 2024–25 ([tyt.com.mx](https://www.tyt.com.mx/nota/autotransporte-mexico-eu-supera-por-primera-vez-los-50000-mdd-en-valor-de-carga), [transporte.mx](https://transporte.mx/asi-se-ha-comportado-el-movimiento-de-carga-entre-mexico-y-estados-unidos-desde-el-2000/)). The US–Mexico cross-border FTL market is forecast to grow at ~5–7% CAGR on nearshoring ([mordorintelligence.com](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/us-mexico-cross-border-ftl-freight-transport-market), [emergemarket.com](https://www.emergemarket.com/post/why-nearshoring-is-reshaping-u-s--mexico-freight)).

**Digitalization maturity is low at the long tail.** ELDs are mandated (see §4), but most small carriers still run paper-driven dispatch, manual billing, and spreadsheet accounting; freight tech adoption correlates with fleet size, and TMS penetration remains modest among sub-20-truck fleets ([ti-insight.com](https://www.ti-insight.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Road-Transport-SOLS-2024-WP-3.pdf), [rxo.com](https://rxo.com/resources/carrier/5-carrier-steps/)).

## 2. Driver Pain Points

1. **ELD/HOS compliance pressure.** Roughly **3.5 million US drivers** are subject to hours-of-service rules ([everycrsreport.com](https://www.everycrsreport.com/files/20200318_R46276_c5065639f9ee9d8a60a2456ae212725636c70d50.html)). ELD data made driving-time enforcement strict and unforgiving; drivers report logbook stress, coercion fears, and reduced schedule flexibility ([worktruckonline.com](https://www.worktruckonline.com/321964/the-unintended-consequences-of-the-eld-mandate), [pressdemocrat.com](https://www.pressdemocrat.com/2017/08/09/federal-mandate-for-electronic-logs-divides-truckers/)).
2. **Detention at shippers/receivers.** ATRI's 2019 study found **average detention of ~140 minutes per stop, 61 million hours lost industry-wide, worth ~$6.2 billion** in lost productivity ([truckingresearch.org](https://truckingresearch.org/2019/09/new-atri-study-quantifies-driver-detention-impacts/)); conditions have worsened since ([ccjdigital.com](https://www.ccjdigital.com/business/article/14938792/excessive-detention-getting-worse-says-atri)). Newer estimates peg the industry cost near **$15B/year** ([innovativelogisticsgroup.io](https://innovativelogisticsgroup.io/profitability-and-cost-control/driver-detention-costs-the-industry-15-billion-a-year-how-small-carriers-build-a-real-accessorial-strategy-in-2026/)).
3. **Truck parking shortage — drivers' #1 concern.** ATRI surveys repeatedly rank parking among the top issues ([truckdriversus.com](https://truckdriversus.com/truck-parking-remains-top-concern-for-drivers-atri-survey-reveals/)). The US has ~**313,000 truck parking spaces** but an **estimated ~40,000-space overnight shortfall**, leaving drivers to park illegally or in unsafe spots ([truckparkingclub.com](https://truckparkingclub.com/news/how-many-truck-spots-are-in-us/), [parking-mobility-magazine.org](https://parking-mobility-magazine.org/online-exclusive/bridging-the-truck-parking-gap/)).
4. **Paperwork and BOLs.** Drivers still handle paper bills of lading, rate confirmations, lumper receipts, and proof-of-delivery photos; document capture and back-office reconciliation are manual for most small carriers ([sdcexec.com](https://www.sdcexec.com/transportation/trucking/article/22928262/scanbot-sdk-is-driver-technology-keeping-up)).
5. **Broker communication and unreliable commitments.** Drivers lose pay when brokers' promised loads fall through, rates get repriced, or nobody answers the phone; FMCSA has had to force broker transparency (e.g., Uber Freight) after double-brokering scams ([overdriveonline.com](https://www.overdriveonline.com/regulations/article/15668045/fmcsa-forces-transparency-from-uber-freight-after-scam-uncovered)).
6. **Pay disputes and slow, disputed settlements.** Pay-per-mile settlements vary; detention, layover, and tarp pay are routinely disputed or unpaid, and driver time isn't valued ([landline.media](https://landline.media/drivers-time-not-valued-ooida-says-in-detention-time-comments/)).
7. **CSA scores and safety anxiety.** Drivers worry their CSA (Compliance, Safety, Accountability) scores and violations affect job prospects; a single inspection can change employment eligibility ([geotab.com](https://www.geotab.com/blog/csa-score/)).
8. **Fuel and operating cost pressure.** Diesel averaged ~$3.50/gal at end-2024 ([worktruckonline.com](https://www.worktruckonline.com/news/diesel-average-ends-2024-at-350)); fuel is typically 25–35% of operating cost, and fuel card/fueling decisions are a daily driver burden.
9. **Truck stop conditions and amenities.** Shower availability, security, and restroom conditions at truck stops are a consistent quality-of-life complaint; NATSO tracks how trucker apps surface these amenities ([natso.com](https://www.natso.com/get-more-exposure-with-trucker-apps/)).
10. **Isolation, health, and retention drivers.** Long-haul work is associated with high turnover and wellbeing challenges; family benefits and wellness support are emerging retention levers ([selerix.com](https://selerix.com/blog/beyond-the-paycheck-the-role-of-family-benefits-in-boosting-truck-driver-wellness-loyalty/)).

## 3. Hauler / Fleet Owner Pain Points

1. **Finding profitable loads.** Load boards are the default for small fleets, but rates are volatile and capacity is fragmented; carriers struggle to fill backhauls ([usatruckerchoice.com](https://usatruckerchoice.com/reviews/load-boards/), [idtpakistan.pk](https://idtpakistan.pk/blog/best-load-boards-for-dispatchpers-2025/)).
2. **Empty miles (deadhead).** Industry average deadhead runs **~15–20% of miles**; cutting deadhead with matching/routing is a top ROI lever ([fleetworks.ai](https://www.fleetworks.ai/resources/deadhead-miles-trucking), [bringoz.com](https://www.bringoz.com/how-to-reduce-empty-miles-in-freight-operations/)).
3. **Freight fraud and double-brokering.** CargoNet recorded **3,625 cargo-theft cases in 2024 with losses surpassing ~$455 million** across the US and Canada ([landline.media](https://landline.media/cargo-theft-loss-reaches-nearly-half-a-billion-in-2024/), [trucknews.com](https://www.trucknews.com/transportation/freight-fraud-losses-surpass-455m-in-2024/)); double-brokering and fake-carrier scams are a Senate-hearing-level concern ([tianet.org](https://news.tianet.org/senate-commerce-hearing-highlights-freight-fraud-join-tias-efforts-for-stronger-enforcement/)).
4. **Factoring fees and slow payment.** Most small carriers factor invoices at **1–5% fees** or wait 30–60+ days for net terms, squeezing already-thin margins ([truckfirstclass.com](https://truckfirstclass.com/invoice-factoring-cost-trucking/), [merituscapital.com](https://www.merituscapital.com/blog/truck-factoring-companies)).
5. **Insurance costs.** Commercial auto insurance hit **~$0.102/mile in 2024** and renewals worsened in 2025 amid nuclear verdicts ([ninjatms.com](https://ninjatms.com/blog/insurance-cost-hit-0-102-per-mile-in-2024-and-2025-renewals-worse/), [truckinginfo.com](https://www.truckinginfo.com/10229818/nuclear-verdicts-insurance-cost-electric-vehicles-rise-in-atri-top-industry-issu)).
6. **Driver recruitment and retention.** Large-fleet annualized turnover runs **~90–94%**; recruitment costs and training churn are among the largest controllable expenses ([landline.media](https://landline.media/magazine/ooida-foundation-shines-light-on-driver-turnover-problem/), [rapidrelay.ai](https://rapidrelay.ai/white-papers/driver-retention-is-a-data-problem)).
7. **Maintenance and total cost per mile.** ATRI's operational cost research puts average marginal cost near **$2.27/mile** (with insurance, maintenance, tires, fuel) ([fleetowner.com](https://www.fleetowner.com/operations/article/55302561/for-hire-trucking-became-less-profitable-in-2024-according-to-atris-cost-of-trucking-report)).
8. **ELD/compliance administration.** Managing ELD data, HOS violations, driver qualification files, and DOT audits is a dedicated back-office burden for small fleets that can't afford compliance staff ([cargocredible.com](https://cargocredible.com/blog/eld-compliance-hours-of-service-hos-auditing-2025/)).
9. **Brokerage transparency and rate fairness.** Carriers face opaque broker markup, last-minute rate cuts, and lack of leverage; FMCSA has proposed broker record-keeping/transparency rules ([fccr.co](https://fccr.co/broker-transparency-faces-potential-new-rules-from-the-fmcsa/)).
10. **The freight recession and carrier failures.** 2024–25 saw record carrier and broker failures as rates collapsed ([freightwaves.com](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/bankruptcies-closures-and-fraud-key-trucking-stories-in-2024), [magazine.factoring.org](https://magazine.factoring.org/magazine-articles/carrier-amp-broker-failures-in-20242025-and-why-2026-may-bring-one-last-wave)).

## 4. Regulation & Compliance — and the Software Needs They Create

- **US ELD mandate (FMCSA).** Required ELDs for nearly all CMV drivers since **Dec 18, 2017**, full enforcement Dec 2019; ~3.5M drivers affected ([fmcsa.dot.gov](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/hours-service/elds/eld-fact-sheet-english-version), [everycrsreport.com](https://www.everycrsreport.com/files/20200318_R46276_c5065639f9ee9d8a60a2456ae212725636c70d50.html)). **Software need:** certified ELD logging, HOS status, 8/14/60/70-day recap, roadside-inspection view, and FMCSA-compliant data transfer.
- **FMCSA Hours-of-Service (2020 final rule).** Expanded flexibility: 30-min break, split sleeper-berth, adverse-driving-condition provisions ([fmcsa.dot.gov](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/newsroom/us-department-transportation-modernizes-hours-service-rules-improve-safety-and-increase)). **Software need:** HOS simulators/predictors so drivers know when they can legally run; automated break reminders.
- **IFTA (International Fuel Tax Agreement).** Carriers crossing state/province lines file **quarterly IFTA fuel-tax reports** allocating fuel purchases across jurisdictions ([fleetowner.com](https://www.fleetowner.com/perspectives/ideaxchange/blog/55172896/a-fleet-operators-guide-to-the-international-fuel-tax-agreement), [truckx.com](https://truckx.com/faqs/ifta/what-is-an-ifta-fuel-tax-report/)). **Software need:** automated mileage-per-jurisdiction capture from GPS/ELD + fuel-card data, generating audit-ready IFTA returns.
- **Canada ELD mandate.** Federal ELD rules took effect **June 2021**, with provincial enforcement rolling out through 2023 (Québec Apr 2023, etc.) for both federally and provincially regulated carriers ([millerthomson.com](https://www.millerthomson.com/en/insights/transportation-and-logistics/eld-enforcement-begin-quebec-april-30-2023-motor-carriers/), [cantruck.ca](https://cantruck.ca/fr/rollout-of-eld-mandates-continue-across-canada/), [lexology.com](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=71591458-574e-4c70-b380-b6059a8565aa)). **Software need:** a single ELD/telematics platform covering US + Canadian rule sets and HOS regimes (US HOS vs Canadian CTA hours).
- **Mexico Carta Porte.** Since 2022, SAT requires the **Carta Porte complement** (CFDI 4.0/3.1) on all freight invoices — origin/destination, cargo, route, vehicle, operator — with fines up to ~**MXN 112,000+** for non-compliance ([nuvocargo.com](https://www.nuvocargo.com/blog-posts/complemento-carta-porte-what-to-look-out-for-to-avoid-penalties-and-fines), [debate.com.mx](https://www.debate.com.mx/consejos/SAT-te-MULTARA-con-hasta-112000-en-2025-si-no-tienes-este-DOCUMENTO-OBLIGATORIO-20250626-0143.html), [tradeway.mx](https://tradeway.mx/en/blog/carta-porte-31-what-it-is-penalties/)). **Software need:** bilingual CFDI/Carta Porte document generation tied to shipment lifecycle, plus border-document bundles (pedimento, I-94 style manifests).
- **Mexico NOM-087-SICT-2-2024.** New driving-time and rest rules for federal autotransporte (published Oct 2024) — codifying max driving hours and mandated pauses, enforceable via tachograph/geolocation ([dofnot.com](https://dofnot.com/nota/20241017-proyecto-de-norma-oficial-mexicana-nom-087-sict-2-2024-que-establece-los-tiempos), [canacosolucioneslegales.com.mx](https://www.canacosolucioneslegales.com.mx/norma-oficial-nom-087-sict-2-2024/)). **Software need:** Mexican duty-time logging akin to ELD — a differentiator vs. US-only ELD vendors.

**Bottom line:** compliance is the forcing function for adoption. Any serious North American platform must be **tri-country**: US FMCSA/ELD + IFTA, Canada ELD/HOS, Mexico Carta Porte/NOM-087 — and cross-border document automation is the wedge.

## 5. Existing Competitors

**Telematics / ELD platforms (asset side):**
- **Samsara** — the category leader: FY2025 revenue ~**$1.42B**, ARR ~$1.5B, 24,000+ customers; connected operations (ELD, dashcams, GPS) ([nasdaq.com](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/samsara-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-fiscal-year-2025-financial-results-2025-03-06)). Weakness: hardware-centric, priced for mid-market fleets; load matching, brokerage, and cross-border are not core.
- **Motive** — AI dashcams + ELD + fleet ops; **~$500M ARR**, filed S-1 (Dec 2025) ([saastr.com](https://www.saastr.com/5-interesting-learnings-from-motive-at-500000000-arr-and-the-match-up-with-samsara/), [cnbc.com](https://www.cnbc.com/amp/2025/12/23/alphabet-backed-motive-files-for-ipo.html)). Weakness: same hardware/telematics lane; driver-app UX criticized; not a dispatch/load platform.
- **Geotab** — largest telematics install base (4M+ vehicles globally); ABI #1 ranking ([abiresearch.com](https://www.abiresearch.com/press/geotab-samsara-and-powerfleet-take-the-top-spots-in-abis-commercial-telematics-competitive-ranking)). Weakness: OEM-grade data platform, not a driver workflow app; weak on freight business processes.
- **Verizon Connect / Omnitracs (Solera)** — legacy ELD/telematics and TMS; large installed base, dated UX ([trustradius.com](https://www.trustradius.com/compare-products/omnitracs-one-vs-verizon-connect)).

**Load matching / marketplaces:**
- **DAT** — dominant load board (Roper-owned); acquired Trucker Tools (Dec 2024) for real-time tracking ([ayondo.com](https://www.ayondo.com/en/bwr/US7766961061/roper-technologies/dat-acquires-trucker-tools-an-innovator-in-real-time-load-tracking-and-visibility-20241217380802r1)). Weakness: subscription load-board, per-load fees; no operations backbone for small fleets.
- **Truckstop** — #2 load board (Roper, ~$1.1B acquisition); integrated with Trucker Path ([freightwaves.com](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/trucker-path-truckstop-integration)). Weakness: same transactional model.
- **Uber Freight** — digital brokerage; revenue sliding and **EBITDA still negative** despite scale ([freightwaves.com](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/more-ebitda-losses-at-uber-freight-but-ron-touts-brokerage-tms-integration)). Lesson: pure-play digital brokerage is hard to make profitable.
- **Loadsmart** — raised $200M+ at ~$800M valuation for digital FTL booking; still niche ([salestools.io](https://salestools.io/en/report/loadsmart-200m-series-unknown)).
- **Trucker Path** — the driver-side app with **1M+ users**, parking info, and a free load board; acquired/partnered broadly ([freightwaves.com](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/trucker-path-truckstop-integration), [thetrucker.com](https://www.thetrucker.com/trucking-news/business/truck-parking-club-joins-the-trucker-path-marketplace)). Weakness: driver tool, not a fleet back-office system; monetization via subscriptions/marketplace ads.

**TMS / enterprise:**
- **McLeod Software** — LoadMaster TMS for mid/large carriers; deep but heavy, desktop-era UX ([digitalpermitbook.com](https://www.digitalpermitbook.com/post/why-is-mcleod-trucking-software-still-the-most-popular-tms-why-do-enterprise-carriers-choose-them)).
- **Trimble Transportation** — TMS + PeopleNet ELD/telematics; enterprise-grade, expensive ([usatruckerchoice.com](https://usatruckerchoice.com/head-to-head/mcleod-software-vs-tms-systems-trimble/)).
- **project44 / FourKites** — real-time visibility for shippers (the *demand* side), not tools for small carriers ([gartner.com](https://www.gartner.com/reviews/market/real-time-transportation-visibility-platforms/vendor/trucker-tools/alternatives)).
- **Drivrz** — newer Android/iOS driver + carrier load app targeting owner-operators; small footprint ([play.google.com](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=driv.cgweb.ca)).

**The Convoy collapse (Oct 2023) — the market's biggest lesson.** Convoy raised **~$1.1B**, peaked at a **$3.8B valuation**, and shut down within weeks, having never proven unit economics ([fortune.com](https://fortune.com/2023/10/19/convoy-trucking-tech-startup-shuts-down-jeff-bezos-investor/), [axios.com](https://www.axios.com/local/seattle/2023/10/26/convoy-trucking-company-closing-why-explained)). Lessons: (1) asset-light "Uber for trucking" brokerage doesn't work at scale without a durable supply-side relationship; (2) subsidized digital freight matching collapses when freight rates drop; (3) freight tech must sell software to fleets (recurring SaaS), not just intermediate freight — that's why Samsara/Motive (SaaS) survived while Convoy/Loadsmart-style brokerages struggled ([spglobal.com](https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/research/a-decade-on-logistics-industry-still-awaits-proof-that-vc-is-a), [inc.com](https://www.inc.com/issie-lapowsky/when-convoy-collapsed-the-uber-for-trucking-left-behind-collateral-damage.html)).

## 6. Market Gaps — Where a New Android+SaaS Player Wins

1. **Small fleets (1–20 trucks) and owner-operators are underserved.** 95.5% of US carriers are ≤10 trucks, but enterprise TMS/telematics pricing, hardware requirements, and complexity exclude them ([motor.com](https://www.motor.com/category/fleets/page/10/)). A **mobile-first, no-hardware-required (BYOD) ELD + dispatch + back-office** stack priced per truck/month can own this tail — Samsara/Motive need $500+ hardware installs; a pure-app play doesn't.
2. **Driver-first UX is a genuine differentiator.** Incumbents build for fleet managers; drivers complain about clunky ELD apps. A driver app that genuinely reduces workload — digital BOLs, one-tap POD, detention timers, in-app settlement visibility — improves retention, and retention is the carrier's #1 cost lever (~90%+ turnover) ([rapidrelay.ai](https://rapidrelay.ai/white-papers/driver-retention-is-a-data-problem), [landline.media](https://landline.media/magazine/ooida-foundation-shines-light-on-driver-turnover-problem/)).
3. **Cross-border US–Mexico brokerage/ops is under-tooled.** Record cross-border volumes (>$50B/month) meet a market of fragmented drayage carriers, paper Carta Porte workflows, and US-only software ([tyt.com.mx](https://www.tyt.com.mx/nota/autotransporte-mexico-eu-supera-por-primera-vez-los-50000-mdd-en-valor-de-carga), [nuvocargo.com](https://www.nuvocargo.com/blog-posts/complemento-carta-porte-what-to-look-out-for-to-avoid-penalties-and-fines)). A **bilingual (EN/ES) platform with Carta Porte + US ELD + border-document bundles** has almost no direct competitor; Nuvocargo addresses brokerage, not fleet operations.
4. **Parking and driver-lifestyle data is monetizable.** Parking is the #1 driver concern and Trucker Path monetizes it — but it's a standalone app. An integrated ops platform can surface real-time parking + fuel + detention data *inside* the dispatch workflow ([truckdriversus.com](https://truckdriversus.com/truck-parking-remains-top-concern-for-drivers-atri-survey-reveals/), [natso.com](https://www.natso.com/get-more-exposure-with-trucker-apps/)).
5. **Empty-mile matching for the long tail.** Load boards are transactional; no player tightly couples deadhead reduction with the carrier's own ELD/GPS data for small fleets. Automated backhaul suggestions from your own fleet's position is an untapped wedge ([fleetworks.ai](https://www.fleetworks.ai/resources/deadhead-miles-trucking)).
6. **Detention/accessorial automation.** Detention costs ~$15B/yr but small carriers rarely bill it because documenting wait time is manual. ELD-time-stamped detention capture + auto-invoicing is a concrete, defensible feature ([innovativelogisticsgroup.io](https://innovativelogisticsgroup.io/profitability-and-cost-control/driver-detention-costs-the-industry-15-billion-a-year-how-small-carriers-build-a-real-accessorial-strategy-in-2026/)).
7. **Freight-fraud vetting.** With $455M+ in 2024 fraud losses, a platform that pre-vets brokers/carriers (FMCSA data + payment history + identity checks) inside the dispatch flow is high-value trust infrastructure ([trucknews.com](https://www.trucknews.com/transportation/freight-fraud-losses-surpass-455m-in-2024/), [tianet.org](https://news.tianet.org/senate-commerce-hearing-highlights-freight-fraud-join-tias-efforts-for-stronger-enforcement/)).
8. **SaaS-over-brokerage model.** Convoy proved brokerage-alone fails; the winning model is **software subscription with optional freight/factoring/insurance revenue**, matching Samsara/Motive's durability while serving fleets the incumbents ignore ([spglobal.com](https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/research/a-decade-on-logistics-industry-still-awaits-proof-that-vc-is-a)).

## 7. Recommended MVP

**Wedge: a driver-first, BYOD (no hardware) tri-country operations platform for fleets of 1–20 trucks, starting in the US, with a Mexico cross-border module as phase 2.**

**Android app (driver side) — prioritized:**
1. **Certified FMCSA ELD logging + HOS predictor** (24/7 recap, break/sleeper-berth logic, inspection mode) — the compliance hook that makes drivers install the app ([fmcsa.dot.gov](https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/hours-service/elds/eld-fact-sheet-english-version)).
2. **Digital BOL + one-tap POD** (photo + GPS + timestamp signature) — kills the paperwork pain point and feeds invoicing.
3. **Detention/layover timer** — auto-stamps wait time at shippers and generates accessorial claims ([truckingresearch.org](https://truckingresearch.org/2019/09/new-atri-study-quantifies-driver-detention-impacts/)).
4. **Load & dispatch inbox** — assigned loads, rate confirmations, appointment windows, documents, in-app chat with dispatcher.
5. **Parking + fuel + weigh-station layer** (API feeds like Trucker Path/Drivewyze data) — the daily-life utility that drives retention ([natso.com](https://www.natso.com/get-more-exposure-with-trucker-apps/)).
6. **Settlement visibility** — driver sees per-load pay, deductions, and detention pay accruing (retention killer feature).

**SaaS dashboard (fleet/dispatcher side) — prioritized:**
1. **Dispatch board** — drag-and-drop load-to-truck assignment with live ELD/GPS status.
2. **Document inbox → invoicing** — auto-builds invoices from PODs/detention stamps; QuickBooks/Xero sync; optional factoring marketplace ([truckfirstclass.com](https://truckfirstclass.com/invoice-factoring-cost-trucking/)).
3. **IFTA + fuel module** — automated jurisdiction mileage from ELD data for quarterly reports ([fleetowner.com](https://www.fleetowner.com/perspectives/ideaxchange/blog/55172896/a-fleet-operators-guide-to-the-international-fuel-tax-agreement)).
4. **Broker/carrier vetting** — FMCSA authority, insurance, and payment-score checks at booking time (fraud defense) ([tianet.org](https://news.tianet.org/senate-commerce-hearing-highlights-freight-fraud-join-tias-efforts-for-stronger-enforcement/)).
5. **Empty-mile matching** — backhaul suggestions from the fleet's live positions and load-board feeds ([bringoz.com](https://www.bringoz.com/how-to-reduce-empty-miles-in-freight-operations/)).
6. **Compliance inbox** — driver qualification files, inspection/violation flags, ELD data-transfer to auditors.

**Phase 2 (region wedge): Mexico module** — Carta Porte 3.1 generation, bilingual UI, NOM-087 duty logging, border document bundles, and US–MX drayage workflows ([nuvocargo.com](https://www.nuvocargo.com/blog-posts/complemento-carta-porte-what-to-look-out-for-to-avoid-penalties-and-fines), [tradeway.mx](https://tradeway.mx/en/blog/carta-porte-31-what-it-is-penalties/)).

**Pricing:** $30–50/truck/month SaaS (undercuts Samsara/Motive hardware bundles), plus 1–2% take on optional factoring/payments. GTM: owner-operator associations, dispatch-service networks, and cross-border drayage brokers.

## Summary

The single biggest opportunity for RoadwiseFleet in North America is **owning the underserved small-fleet (1–20 trucks) driver-first operations layer: a no-hardware Android ELD + dispatch + back-office SaaS for the ~95% of carriers too small for Samsara/Motive/McLeod, then extending it into the booming, under-tooled US–Mexico cross-border corridor** — where record >$50B/month truck trade, mandatory Carta Porte compliance, and a 90,000-driver deficit create a bilingual market almost no incumbent serves. The Convoy collapse proved pure digital brokerage fails; the durable model is recurring SaaS that reduces driver workload, automates compliance (ELD/IFTA/Carta Porte), and digitizes the paperwork that still dominates small-fleet haulage today.
