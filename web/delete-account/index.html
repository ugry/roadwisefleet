Delete your RoadwiseFleet account

You can delete your account here, on the web, without the mobile app — or from More → Delete account inside the app. Both paths do exactly the same thing.

What happens when you delete

Your account is anonymised immediately. Your name, e-mail address, phone number, password and two-factor secret are erased, every phone that was signed in to your account is revoked, and you are signed out everywhere.

Some records have to stay, because they document work that was already carried out:

  • Trip records — routes, stops, status history and rates, kept as the carrier's operational record.
  • Delivery evidence — proof of delivery, eCMR, invoice scans and photos.
  • Reviews you already gave or received, shown against the carrier, not your name.

Those records are no longer linked to your name. How long they are kept is set by the carrier's accounting and legal obligations; we do not delete them on request because they evidence work performed and amounts invoiced.

Confirm it is you

This cannot be undone. There is no way to recover an anonymised account.
