The problem

Trucking runs on paperwork, not progress

Small fleets lose time and money to the same three leaks. RoadwiseFleet plugs them with software drivers actually use.

Paperwork chaos

Every load spawns a folder — CMR, POD, e-way bills, invoices. Drivers photograph them, dispatchers retype them, auditors chase them later.

We fix it: digital trip cards capture each document once, at the source.

Empty miles & deadhead

Trucks come home empty because no one can see where capacity is. Every deadhead mile is fuel burned and revenue lost.

We fix it: live positions and a shared board make the next load visible before the current one ends.

Slow payments & churn

Drivers wait 60–90 days to get paid, then leave for whoever pays faster. Churn — not fuel — is your most expensive line item.

We fix it: a per-driver money ledger and quick-pay turn waiting into cash.

For drivers · Android

An app your drivers will actually use

Phone-first and built for the cab — not a dashboard squeezed onto a screen. It works on the road, even in low-signal areas.

Digital trip cards & POD

Trip details, documents, and proof of delivery in one card. Sign on glass.

GPS check-ins

Automatic check-in at pickup and delivery. No more “where are you?” calls.

Dispatch chat

WhatsApp-style messaging with your dispatcher, attached to the trip — not lost in another app.

Money ledger

Every load, rate, and payment — visible to the driver in real time.

Compliance helpers

eCMR, e-way bill, CT-e, ELD — the region’s rules, with forms pre-filled.

SOS & secure parking

One-tap emergency alerts and vetted secure parking nearby.

For owners & dispatchers · Web

The dashboard your dispatcher will live in

Dispatch, documents, and money in one view — no separate TMS, telematics box, and spreadsheet.

Dispatch board

Assign trips to drivers. See who’s loaded, who’s empty, and who’s running late.

Live map

Every truck and every load on one map, with route history and geofenced check-ins.

Document vault

Every CMR, invoice, and POD — searchable, and audit-ready when the tax authority asks.

Trip P&L

Fuel, tolls, detention, and payout per trip — know what each load actually earned.

Driver scorecards

On-time, safety, and idle time per driver. Fair, transparent, and motivating.

Quick-pay & invoicing

Send invoices and pay drivers in days — not months.
