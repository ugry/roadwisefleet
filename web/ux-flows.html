1 · Company types & who exists inside them — the missing dimension

Four operator types plus the customer side. The same app serves all of them, but authority differs: a solo owner acts alone, a micro fleet needs requests between owner and drivers, a small fleet adds dispatcher and accountant, and a partner company is a second org touching the same trip.

2 · Request catalogue — what each party may ask for — and what the platform decides

Every non-authoritative action becomes a request that the platform validates, times, escalates and records. The platform row is the shared machinery: policy check, SLA timer, notify-only-after-decision, audit and webhook fan-out.

3 · Entry points — where each party gets into the system — and what leaves it

Customers enter via portal, WhatsApp or their own ERP (API). Fleets via the dashboard. Drivers via the Android app. Partners via their own org's dashboard. Everything lands on one trip object; what leaves is notifications, webhooks, push and alerts — never raw state changes.

4 · Customer journey — quote or book, track, change, cancel, dispute, pay

Two branches matter for policy: change request (fleet may counter-propose) and cancellation request (fee policy applies after dispatch — decided by the fleet, not the customer). The customer only ever sees the decided outcome.

5 · Fleet manager flow — an inbox of requests plus own-authority actions

The manager's day is inbound: driver requests (cancel, reassign, expense, advance, delay), customer asks, system alerts (detention, missing documents, expiring files, expiring SLAs) and partner updates — each with reason, evidence, SLA countdown and customer-impact preview. Approve or decline, and only then does anyone outside the org hear about it.

6 · Driver flow — execute the trip, request everything else

The driver acts freely on execution (start, deliver, upload POD) and requests anything that changes a commitment (cancel, reassign, expense, advance). Employed drivers request from their dispatcher; solo owner-operators accept loads directly. While a request is pending the driver remains responsible — the app must say so.

7 · Information flow v2 — the owner decisions of 2026-10-01 (#73)

The master flow after the owner's product decisions: every transacting user is registered (no guest booking; share links are read-only), payment per award is carrier invoice or escrow 3%, verification is optional with ID / Licence / Registration check marks, reviews are sampled at ≤1 per 10 actions, auto-match has no limits, and the marketplace is named Hauling Market.

8 · Menus v2 — menus keyed by account type

Fleet manager (12 items, incl. Hauling Market and Billing and Plan), customer portal (Payments shows invoice OR escrow 3%, plus sampled Reviews), solo driver (Hauling Market feed, verification check marks, two-way reviews), the employed driver app, and the read-only unregistered-party link.
