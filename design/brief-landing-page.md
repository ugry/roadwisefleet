# RoadwiseFleet — Landing Page Design Brief (for OpenDesign)

## Brand
- **Product name:** RoadwiseFleet
- **Domain:** roadwisefleet.com
- **One-liner:** The operating system for small trucking fleets and owner-operators.
- **What it is:** An Android app for truck drivers + a SaaS web dashboard for fleet owners and dispatchers. Phone-first, no hardware boxes needed.

## Positioning
- Driver-first: the product reduces driver workload (paperwork, slow payments, bad communication) — this is also the retention story, because the industry has a structural driver shortage.
- For small carriers (1–20 trucks) and owner-operators, who are ignored by enterprise TMS and telematics vendors.
- One platform, regional superpowers: eCMR/eFTI compliance (Europe), e-way bill + FASTag + UPI (India), CT-e/MDF-e + Pix (Brazil), ELD/HOS (North America).

## Hero
- Headline options: "The operating system for small trucking fleets." or "Run your fleet from the driver's seat."
- Subheadline: mention driver app + dispatch dashboard + compliance + faster payments, for 1–20-truck fleets.
- CTA: "Get early access" (waitlist email capture) + secondary "See how it works".
- Visual: dual mockup — Android driver app phone next to a SaaS dashboard screen, on a dark asphalt/map motif with route lines.

## Sections (single landing page)
1. **Nav** — logo "RoadwiseFleet", links: Product, Drivers, Fleets, Pricing, plus CTA button.
2. **Hero** (above).
3. **Stats strip** — industry pain stats from research: "140-min average detention" / "15–20% empty miles" / "60–90-day payment terms" / "~233k missing drivers in Europe" — framed as problems we solve.
4. **Problem** — three cards: Paperwork chaos · Empty miles & deadhead · Slow payments & driver churn.
5. **Product: Driver app (Android)** — feature grid: digital trip cards & POD, GPS check-ins, WhatsApp-style dispatch chat, money ledger, compliance helpers, SOS & secure parking.
6. **Product: Fleet dashboard (SaaS)** — dispatch board, live map, document vault, trip P&L, driver scorecards, quick-pay/invoicing.
7. **Regional compliance/payments strip** — EU eCMR · India e-way bill/UPI · Brazil CT-e/Pix · NA ELD — small badges.
8. **How it works** — 3 steps: Install the app → Invite your drivers → Run better loads.
9. **Pricing teaser** — "Per truck, per month. No hardware. No lock-in." with a CTA (no prices yet).
10. **Final CTA + waitlist form** — email input, "Join the early-access list".
11. **Footer** — logo, tagline, links (Product, Drivers, Fleets, Contact), © RoadwiseFleet.

## Audience
Fleet owners, owner-operators, dispatchers. Trustworthy, modern, slightly industrial (road/freight) but clean SaaS aesthetic.

## Tone
Confident, plain-spoken, benefit-led. English copy. Avoid hype; lead with workload reduction and money.

## Technical
Single HTML file, Tailwind (CDN ok) or hand-rolled CSS, responsive, dark hero + light sections, one or two accent colors (e.g., deep navy + safety amber). Real content, no lorem ipsum. Include phone/dashboard mockups built in CSS/SVG.
